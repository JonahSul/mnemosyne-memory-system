All agents must periodically initiate synchronization checkpoints during collaborative workflows—especially after major code, rule, or discovery updates. At each checkpoint, agents must verify current alignment with collaboration goals, incorporate new cross-agent discoveries, and ensure that no critical updates require halting or reorientation.
