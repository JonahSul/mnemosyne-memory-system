# Go-To-Market: Enterprise AI/Automation (Mnemosyne)

Audience: Intermediate technical professionals (engineering managers, solutions architects, security/COMPLIANCE leads) involved in enterprise adoption.

Objective
- Establish a repeatable, outcome-focused GTM strategy for enterprise buyers spanning awareness to renewal.
- Emphasize governance, provenance, and risk mitigation as core differentiators.

Scope
- Covers positioning, ICPs, packaging, pilot strategy, enablement, and governance-focused KPIs.

Step-by-step
1) Align executive sponsor and GTM owner
- Secure charter, success criteria, and budget boundaries for the first 12 months.
- Document decision rights and escalation paths.
2) Define ICPs and personas
- Primary: CIO/CTO, VP of AI/Automation, Data & Security leads, Procurement with security focus.
- Secondary: Cloud Architects, Platform Owners, Compliance Officers.
3) Craft value narrative and proof points
- Governance at scale, provenance, auto-arbitration readiness, auditable decisions.
- ROI levers: risk reduction, automation uplift, time-to-value.
4) Package and pricing blueprint
- Core OSS with governance addons; hosted SaaS with multi-tenant governance dashboards; on-prem options; services.
- Early-stage pricing scaffolding: seat-based, usage-based, and governance-module tiers.
5) Pilot strategy
- Define 1–2 enterprise use cases per pilot; success criteria; data access & security approvals.
- Predefine success metrics (time-to-value, risk reduction, cadence of governance events).
6) Asset plan
- Prepare a library of patient assets: ROI calculators, reference architectures, security templates, case studies.
7) Launch plan and governance
- Marketing channels, sales motions, and enablement cadence.
- Define cadence for governance reviews and policy updates.

Artifacts
- GTM charter, ICP matrix, pricing model skeleton, pilot playbook, asset library outline.
