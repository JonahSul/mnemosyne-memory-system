# Enterprise Engagement Model (Mnemosyne)

Audience: GTM program managers, enterprise sales engineers, channel managers

Overview
- Structured engagement from first touch through expansion.
- Emphasize governance, provenance, and risk controls as core value.

Phases
- Phase 1: Awareness to pilot initiation
- Phase 2: Pilot execution with governance depth
- Phase 3: Production deployment with multi-tenant governance
- Phase 4: Expansion and scale

Stakeholders and roles
- Customer stakeholders: CIO/CTO, AI/Automation Lead, Security/Compliance Lead
- Internal stakeholders: GTM Lead, Solutions Architect, SE, Channel Partner Manager

Engagement cadence
- Week 0–2: Discovery call; kickoff.
- Week 2–6: Pilot planning; security approvals.
- Week 6–12: Pilot execution; governance setup.
- Week 12+: Production onboarding and expansion planning.

KPIs and exit criteria
- Pilot success: goals met; governance coverage; audit trail produced.
- Production: time-to-value; governance event cadence; reduction in risk incidents; expansion velocity.
