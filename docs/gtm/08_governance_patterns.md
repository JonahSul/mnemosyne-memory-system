# Governance Patterns (Mnemosyne GTM)

Audience: Enterprise architects and governance leads

Concepts
- Instincts, AXIOMs, Arbiter workﬂows, and provenance tracking
- Risk-aware decision paths and auditability

Patterns
- Surface instincts prior to high-risk actions
- Evidence-based promotion and cross-agent provenance
- Lifecycle management for axioms (promotion, validation, retirement)
- Dispute resolution via Arbiter with persistence of outcomes

Implementation guidance
- Define minimal viable governance surface at first (logging only, no blocking), then progressively enable enforcement
- Integrate with existing security/compliance tooling and dashboards
- Establish a change control process for governance rules and instincts
